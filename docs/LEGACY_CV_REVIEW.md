The latest local Git copies of the English and French CVs were recovered into the private `.artifacts/firebase-migration-baseline/legacy-cvs/` folder. Each language has a PDF and DOCX. Both PDFs have two pages; all four pages were rendered and inspected, and the DOCX text was compared with the paired PDF.

The files are readable and recoverable, but their content predates the current CMS profile:

| Item | Recovered CVs | Current CMS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sunshine employment | July 2025 to present | July 2025 to July 2026 |
| Current El Mouradi role | Not included | Digital Transformation Project Manager from July 2026 |
| Full-time availability | Specifically October 2027 | General international opportunities from 2027 |
| Positioning | Older data-driven marketing headline | Current marketing/commercial analytics and digital transformation positioning |

The English and French copies agree on those older facts. No Italian CV was found in the working tree or local Git objects. ATS and Canadian variants also exist historically; they remain excluded from the public portfolio under the existing resume policy.

**Publication decision:** preserve the recovered documents for review and keep all three CMS CV records unpublished until current files are supplied or the older documents are updated and approved. This prevents the migrated site from offering obsolete downloads. The original CMS rows, asset URLs, and source upload metadata remain in the private source backup/archive.

No CV document was edited or republished during this review.
